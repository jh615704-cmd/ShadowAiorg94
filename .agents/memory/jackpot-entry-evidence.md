name: Jackpot entry evidence
description: Keep Jackpot manual, Auto Best, and AI entries aligned with the six-sample, 58% threshold.
---

Jackpot manual orders, Auto Best, and Jackpot AI require fresh evidence with at least six market samples and an observed rate of at least 58% on every enabled side selected for a manual batch. AI may send only the qualifying contract sides. Market samples and past hit rates are descriptive context, not a promise of future wins.

**Why:** The user requested a 58% observed-rate threshold and six-sample minimum across Jackpot's trade controls.

**How to apply:** Keep the Place button's reason, Auto Best eligibility, and AI candidate selection aligned with the same threshold. Retain freshness checks, account/balance/live-trading checks, session limits, and settlement/uncertain-order safeguards.
