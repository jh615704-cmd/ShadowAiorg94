---
name: Trade contract selection
description: Contract selection, live quote availability, and settlement display rules.
---

For Digit Differs, the selected digit is the barrier that must not be the final digit at expiry; intermediate ticks do not settle the contract. Manual taps must submit that exact barrier, while ranked automation may choose a candidate only when it is explicitly surfaced as ranked mode. Trade X AI Scanner independently chooses the lowest-frequency digit from recent market samples and must send that barrier explicitly, regardless of the manually selected digit; observed frequency is not a prediction or a guarantee. Trade X observation windows must use the next live ticks, not ranked-history positions. Clock Work contracts always last five ticks; Start chooses the best eligible Higher/Lower barrier using the selected win-rate threshold even when the Ai Clock switch is off. Keep this selection logic behind Start, score with available market history, and require one fresh quote to confirm an entry. Bulk Trader sends the selected 1–5 post-entry ticks unchanged for Over, Under, and Differs; tick 0 is the entry spot, and Deriv's sold/settled update is authoritative.

For grouped multi-contract actions (Auto Trader, Bulk Trader, Clock Work, Matches, Dual, and Jackpot), quote every leg first and dispatch the buys together to avoid artificial per-contract delays. Jackpot's selected 1–20 count is the exact total contracts in one batch across enabled families, not a pair count; send that many orders together. Each contract still has its own Deriv acknowledgement, entry quote, and settlement; never promise identical outcomes or an exact shared entry tick. Preserve confirmed contract IDs when only part of a batch is confirmed, and require account-history review before any uncertain order is resubmitted. Features that intentionally wait for settlement before their next entry remain sequential. For Jackpot, preserve the settlement guard, reuse cached market evidence only when its latest tick is no more than 30 seconds old, retain the 50-sample/>68% gates, and keep public market samples across account switches. Stop AI after one acknowledged batch; re-enable it for another scan.

**Why:** Duration-based ranked selection previously replaced a digit the user had selected, and open-contract mark-to-market profit was displayed as a loss before settlement. The user explicitly wants Trade X AI Scanner to choose the coldest observed digit instead of using their manual selection, but no historical signal can guarantee a win. An artificial delay between already-quoted legs also caused grouped entries to start at different market ticks. Waiting for six new quotes to form one five-tick outcome delays Clock Work even when recent market history is already available. A quote can move while a buy request is pending, so users may see more than one digit after pressing Buy even though the configured contract duration is exact. The user wants Clock Work Start to run selection without requiring the Ai Clock switch, does not want explanatory copy about the hidden selection logic in the panel, clarified that Jackpot's count means the exact total contracts for one batch, chose to retain its settlement guard and reuse fresh history, and asked Jackpot AI to stop after one batch.

Matrix's fixed duration menu can contain choices Deriv does not quote for the current market, direction, or stake; a rejected proposal must not be sent as a buy.

**How to apply:** Keep manual and automated entry selection explicit, observe the configured number of fresh ticks before Smart Auto evaluates, and never use an automation setting as the contract duration by accident. For Matrix, check live proposals for the selected market, direction, and stake before enabling durations; never silently replace the user's duration, and refresh the checks after Deriv reports that an offer changed. For Bulk Trader, send the chosen 1–5 duration in both proposal and buy parameters; keep the one-tick post-entry expiry semantics in order logic without displaying the entry-quote/tick-0 explanation, and never synthesize a local settlement from a visible digit. Keep Clock Work fixed at five ticks; pressing Start must run the selected threshold check and barrier choice regardless of the Ai Clock switch, without adding explanatory algorithm copy to Clock Work. Confirm each new entry on one fresh quote; if history or a qualifying setup is unavailable, wait rather than guess. Block overlapping contracts when the feature expects sequential settlement, and display profit only after the contract is closed. For Jackpot, interpret the chosen 1–20 number as the exact order count, quote all selected contracts before sending them together, retain the account-wide settlement guard, and turn AI off after its first acknowledged batch. Auto selection must reject stale or empty tick epochs, keep the 50-sample and strictly-above-68% filters, and skip waiting for another tick when fresh qualifying data is already cached. Clearing Jackpot session history resets the local settled P/L used by its loss limit but never deletes Deriv records or releases open or uncertain-order protections. For grouped batches, preserve per-leg acknowledgements; a rejected or uncertain leg must not hide confirmed siblings or trigger an automatic replay.
