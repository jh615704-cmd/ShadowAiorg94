---
name: Trade contract selection
description: Exact-barrier semantics and settlement display rules for digit trading features.
---

For Digit Differs, the selected digit is the barrier that must not be the final digit at expiry; intermediate ticks do not settle the contract. Manual taps must submit that exact barrier, while ranked automation may choose a candidate only when it is explicitly surfaced as ranked mode. Trade X observation windows must use the next live ticks, not ranked-history positions. Clock Work contracts always last five ticks; Start chooses the best eligible Higher/Lower barrier using the selected win-rate threshold even when the Ai Clock switch is off. Keep this selection logic behind Start, score with available market history, and require one fresh quote to confirm an entry. Bulk Trader sends the selected 1–5 post-entry ticks unchanged for Over, Under, and Differs; tick 0 is the entry spot, and Deriv's sold/settled update is authoritative.

For grouped multi-contract actions (Auto Trader, Bulk Trader, Clock Work, Matches, Dual, and Jackpot), quote every leg first and dispatch the buys together to avoid artificial per-contract delays. Jackpot's selected 1–20 count is the exact total contracts in one batch across enabled families, not a pair count; send that many orders together. Each contract still has its own Deriv acknowledgement, entry quote, and settlement; never promise identical outcomes or an exact shared entry tick. Preserve confirmed contract IDs when only part of a batch is confirmed, and require account-history review before any uncertain order is resubmitted. Features that intentionally wait for settlement before their next entry remain sequential. For Jackpot, the user confirmed that this settlement protection should stay instead of allowing overlapping batches; its Place button should unlock automatically after settlement is confirmed. Jackpot AI should stop after one acknowledged batch; re-enable it for another scan.

**Why:** Duration-based ranked selection previously replaced a digit the user had selected, and open-contract mark-to-market profit was displayed as a loss before settlement. An artificial delay between already-quoted legs also caused grouped entries to start at different market ticks. Waiting for six new quotes to form one five-tick outcome delays Clock Work even when recent market history is already available. A quote can move while a buy request is pending, so users may see more than one digit after pressing Buy even though the configured contract duration is exact. The user wants Clock Work Start to run selection without requiring the Ai Clock switch, does not want explanatory copy about the hidden selection logic in the panel, clarified that Jackpot's count means the exact total contracts for one batch, chose to retain its settlement guard, and asked Jackpot AI to stop after one batch.

**How to apply:** Keep manual and automated entry selection explicit, observe the configured number of fresh ticks before Smart Auto evaluates, and never use an automation setting as the contract duration by accident. For Bulk Trader, send the chosen 1–5 duration in both proposal and buy parameters; keep the one-tick post-entry expiry semantics in order logic without displaying the entry-quote/tick-0 explanation, and never synthesize a local settlement from a visible digit. Keep Clock Work fixed at five ticks; pressing Start must run the selected threshold check and barrier choice regardless of the Ai Clock switch, without adding explanatory algorithm copy to Clock Work. Confirm each new entry on one fresh quote; if history or a qualifying setup is unavailable, wait rather than guess. Block overlapping contracts when the feature expects sequential settlement, and display profit only after the contract is closed. For Jackpot, interpret the chosen 1–20 number as the exact order count, quote all selected contracts before sending them together, retain the account-wide settlement guard, and turn AI off after its first acknowledged batch. Clearing Jackpot session history resets the local settled P/L used by its loss limit but never deletes Deriv records or releases open or uncertain-order protections. For grouped batches, preserve per-leg acknowledgements; a rejected or uncertain leg must not hide confirmed siblings or trigger an automatic replay.
