---
name: Responsive trading controls
description: Project-wide expectation for responsive trading panels and controls.
---

Trading panels and buttons must reflow for the device and viewport in use; controls should not disappear or become inaccessible on laptops or smaller screens. Apply shared sizing and responsive behavior to future trading features too.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked that current and future trading controls adapt to the device for usability, performance, and a neat layout.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing a trading panel, check its desktop/laptop and narrow-screen layout, keep controls within their containers, and preserve usable touch targets.
